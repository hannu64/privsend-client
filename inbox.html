Inbox settings — hold & last-seen

While on hold, your address stops accepting new messages and shows senders a neutral “not accepting messages” page — it does not reveal that you paused it deliberately. Everything already in your inbox stays here to read and delete.

If you opt in, we keep a log — only to weekly accuracy — of when you last unlock and read your inbox. It updates only on a successful unlock with your passphrase, never when the link is merely opened, so coming here to try a passphrase you have lost never signals “active”.
